## Appendix B — Commentary on Revisions

### What I kept from the LLM draft
- I kept the overall section structure (About, Education, Professional Experience, Skills, Projects, Contact).
- I kept the idea of a concise, single-page layout with clear headings and a professional tone.
- I kept the suggestion to include an LLM-use disclosure in an appendix.

### What I changed (and why)
- I replaced all placeholders with my real details (name, university, location, roles, and projects) to ensure factual accuracy.
- I rewrote the About section to reflect my actual interests in strategic management, business analytics, corporate ethics, and my experience as CEO of a restaurant and a sustainable shoe company.
- I changed the professional experience bullets so they describe my real responsibilities and outcomes (for example, 30% year-over-year growth at Zency Kicks).
- I adjusted the wording to sound more like my own communication style and less generic.
- I simplified and clarified some phrases to improve readability and alignment with the course expectations.

### What I removed (and why)
- I removed example bullets that described generic consulting or corporate roles that do not match my background.
- I removed placeholder projects and awards that did not apply to me.
- I removed LLM-specific phrasing that sounded repetitive or overly buzzword-heavy.

I used ChatGPT once to generate an initial outline and sample wording for a one-page MBA portfolio in Quarto.
